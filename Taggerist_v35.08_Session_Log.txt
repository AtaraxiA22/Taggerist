**What was wrong (in plain terms):** Your taglists include short alias words (like `forest`, `PTY_`, `100%`) that the program is supposed to convert into full tags (like `LOC_forest`). The old code did that conversion by searching for those short words *anywhere* in the filename — including inside perfectly good tags. So `LOC_forest` got `LOC_` glued on again (making `LOC_LOC_forest`), `PTY_hat` got caught by the `PTY_` alias (making `PTY_fernshat`), and `whatever` got caught by the `100%` alias (making `wha%_100%ver`). The fix: aliases now only convert when they stand alone as a whole tag between the pipes. Your two exact test filenames now come through unchanged, and I checked all 433 tags in your three lists — none get touched, while all 232 aliases still convert properly.

**Also fixed:** the focus issue you noticed — after clicking a file in the lists, or pressing [SKIP], or the taglist buttons, [Edit tags], [Refresh], and [Help], the cursor now goes to the Search box, like the other buttons.
