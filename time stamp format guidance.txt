Date formats may include: ($ = @/© symbol, ? = wildcard which are always numerals or nulls, never alpha's)

$yy.mmdd-HHMM.?????
$yyyy.mmdd-HHMM.?????
????.mmdd-HHMM.?????

The '.mmdd-HHMM.' format is stable throughout all of the datestamps. This is probably the most reliable and universal indicator of a datestamp: 'dot-4 numbers-hyphen-4 numbers-dot'
