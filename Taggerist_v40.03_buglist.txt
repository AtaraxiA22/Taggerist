#87 - NEW FEATURE  -- forced date formatting
	a.  IF a series of numbers in FilenameEditBox is selected AND the [Fix Date] button is pressed (or keystroke CSA-D is employed), THEN those numbers will be reformatted as xx.xxxx-xxxx.xx
	b. Any non-numerics originally within the selection, such as '-', '/', 'B', etc., are ignored and removed.	
	c. Numerals will be included VERBATIM. I.e. date/time logic does NOT apply. E.g. if the selected number string is '16-18-04-75-97', it will be reformatted as '16.1804-7597' *even if* they exceed the number of months in the year or the number of hours or minutes in HHMM.
	d.  IF there are insufficent # of numerals to form the full xx.xxxx-xxxx.xx string, THEN a partial string will be padded with 0's. E.g. '19-19-13-15' would be reformatted as '19.1913-1500.00'.
	e. Subsequently pressing a CI button will prefix the number string with the CI identifier AND designated as immune to the effects of the [Reduce] button.
